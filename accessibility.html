Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 17, 2026

LOYALTY US is committed to providing a digital experience that is usable by people with disabilities. We aim to design and maintain our public website, business dashboard, QR enrollment flow, and customer wallet with reference to WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Accessibility features

  • Keyboard-accessible links, buttons, and form controls.
  • Visible focus indicators and a skip-to-content link.
  • Programmatic labels for form fields and status announcements for assistive technology.
  • Responsive layouts that support browser zoom and mobile screens.
  • Reduced-motion support for users who request it through their device settings.
  • Text alternatives for functional images such as business QR codes.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Automated checks cannot identify every barrier, so we intend to combine technical testing with manual review and feedback from users.

Report an accessibility problem

If you have difficulty using LOYALTY US, please describe the page, the problem, the assistive technology or browser used, and your preferred way to receive a response. Until a dedicated support address is published, accessibility issues may be reported through the project’s issue-reporting page.

Important: This statement describes our accessibility goal and current features. It is not a certification of complete legal or WCAG conformance. Independent accessibility review is recommended before commercial launch.
